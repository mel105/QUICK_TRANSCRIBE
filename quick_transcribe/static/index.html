Offline transcription

Add a recording (m4a, mp3, wav, mp4 …) with the button on the left or drop it on the page. It is transcribed on this computer and stored in a searchable archive.

  • Click a time to play the recording from that moment.
  • Click the text to correct it; Enter or leaving the line saves. The original is kept.
  • Replace… fixes a misheard name in the whole transcript at once.
  • Add project terms and names to the glossary in Settings; the model then recognises them better.
  • Shortcuts: Space play / pause (outside the text), Ctrl+F search.
